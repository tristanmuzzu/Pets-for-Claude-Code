# v0.7.2: swat it away

One change, reversing one from v0.7.1.

## Clicking a finished card dismisses it again

v0.7.1 made a click on a **Done** card open it instead of dismissing it, on the
reasoning that the moment a card holds the most worth reading should not also
be the moment a click throws it away.

That reasoning was about the wrong thing. Dismissing a finished card is the
gesture this overlay is used with most, dozens of times a day, and it wants to
be a swat at a green rectangle in the corner of the screen — not an aimed click
at an 11-pixel ×. Losing the log was the smaller cost, and it is not really
lost: the session file keeps the last two dozen actions, and the card comes
back on its own the moment that chat starts working again.

So a card that says **Done** goes away completely on a click anywhere on it,
as it did before. Not down to a chip — a chip means something is still going
on.

The distinction v0.7.1 introduced is what makes this safe now. A click only
dismisses a turn that is **genuinely** finished, with nothing it started still
running; a card reading **Finishing · N running** opens like any other working
card, because that turn is not over. Before v0.7.1 the same click would have
swatted away a turn with three subagents still going.

`×` still does the same thing, and still closes a working card down to a
one-line chip.
